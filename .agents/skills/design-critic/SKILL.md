# Senior Design Critic & Aesthetic Audit Framework

## 🎯 Purpose
To conduct rigorous, pixel-level visual and UX critiques of web applications by capturing live headless browser screenshots, analyzing visual hierarchy, typography, spatial rhythm, contrast ratios, and luxury brand alignment.

---

## 🔍 Core Audit Dimensions

### 1. Spatial Rhythm, Layout & Proportions
- **Visual Balance**: Is the composition center-heavy or evenly balanced?
- **Breathing Room**: Do elements have sufficient whitespace (`clamp()`, 8pt grid system)?
- **Card Framing**: Are glass cards properly proportioned without overwhelming screen real estate?

### 2. Typography & Text Hierarchy
- **Font Pairing**: Editorial luxury display font vs clean, legible technical body typeface.
- **Scale & Contrast**: Are `h1`, `h2`, `h3`, badges, and captions clearly distinguishable?
- **Line Length & Leading**: Body text capped at 60–75 characters per line (`max-width: 620px`) with comfortable leading (`1.6–1.75`).

### 3. Color, Lighting & Glassmorphism
- **Color Temperature & Cohesion**: Deep obsidian black (`#070B11`, `#0A0F18`) accented by bioluminescent electric cyan (`#00E5FF`) and royal sapphire (`#0051FF`).
- **Glass Shading**: Smoked glass depth (`backdrop-filter: blur()`, specular rim highlights, subtle linear gradients).
- **Caustics & Glows**: Ambient multi-point backlights that illuminate the background without washing out text readability.

### 4. Background 3D Cosmetic Ecosystem
- **Render Quality**: Specular reflections, crystal glass refraction, active formula luminescence.
- **Depth & Parallax**: Natural, organic spatial constellation with multi-layer depth-of-field blur beads.
- **Performance**: 60–120 FPS GPU composite budget with zero raster hitching.

### 5. Header & Navigation Architecture
- **Hovering Glass Island**: Pill-shaped container, rounded edges, backdrop blur, elevation shadow.
- **Logo Integration**: Exact aspect ratio preservation, crisp edges, balanced CTA pairing.

### 6. Interactive Forms & Conversion Flows
- **Input Styling**: Smoked translucent inputs, subtle focus borders, legible placeholders.
- **Micro-Interactions**: Step transitions, active state radio badges, smooth button hover states.
- **Trust Elements**: NDA badges, response guarantees, reassurance copy.
