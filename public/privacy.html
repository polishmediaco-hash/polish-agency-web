Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026  ·  Last Updated: September 10, 2026

POLISH Media Co ("POLISH", "we", "our", or "us") is a luxury beauty and cosmetics growth agency operating at polishmediaco.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our website, submit a brand application, or sign into our Board Studio portal.

By using our services, you agree to the data practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Authentication data: When you sign in via Google OAuth, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo solely to verify your identity and create your secure session.
  • Application data: Brand name, website URL, monthly revenue, marketing history, goals, and contact details submitted through our partnership application forms.
  • Communication data: Messages and inquiries submitted via contact forms or direct email.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and pages visited, collected automatically to maintain security and improve performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticating and maintaining secure access to our Board Studio portal.
  • Evaluating brand partnership applications and communicating with qualified candidates.
  • Delivering agency services including strategic proposals and campaign collaboration.
  • Improving, securing, and operating our website and internal tools.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

3. Google Sign-In and OAuth 2.0

Our Board Studio uses Google OAuth 2.0 for authentication. When you sign in with Google, we receive only:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • Your Google profile photo

We do not access your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, calendar, or any other Google service. This data is stored securely in our encrypted database and used exclusively to identify you within our platform.

Our use of Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You may revoke access anytime via your Google Account Permissions.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in a secure, encrypted PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase. Our security practices include:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit.
  • Row-level security policies restricting data access to authorized accounts only.
  • JWT authentication tokens with automatic expiry and secure refresh.
  • Administrative access restricted to verified POLISH team members only.

5. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained while your account is active.
  • Partnership application data is retained for up to 2 years from submission.
  • You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 6).

6. Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request permanent deletion of your personal data.
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Withdraw consent: Revoke Google OAuth access at any time without affecting prior processing.

Contact us at contact@polishmediaco.com. We respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

We use strictly necessary browser localStorage to maintain your authenticated session. No third-party advertising cookies, cross-site tracking, or behavioral profiling is used on our website.

8. Third-Party Service Providers

  • Supabase — Database, authentication & storage (supabase.com/privacy)
  • Google OAuth 2.0 — Identity authentication (policies.google.com/privacy)
  • Vercel Inc. — Hosting & CDN (vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy)
  • Calendly — Meeting scheduling (calendly.com/privacy)

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for business professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under 16. If you believe we have done so inadvertently, contact us immediately and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes by posting the revised policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes your acceptance.
