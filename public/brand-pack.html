Brand Standards & Integrity Rules

Preserving visual prestige, optical clear space, and architectural consistency.

Download Brand Book (.MD)
Approved Standard

1X Diamond Clear Space Rule

Maintain an isolation zone around the logo equal to at least 1X, where $X$ is the height of the diamond droplet facet. No text, graphic strokes, or background borders may breach this perimeter.

Approved Standard

Minimum Reproducible Sizing

On digital screens, horizontal lockups must not scale below 120px width; standalone marks not below 24px. In print, horizontal lockups require at least 35mm width.

Strictly Prohibited

No Stretching or Distortion

Never alter the aspect ratio of the arrow stem, droplet facet, or typography. All scaling must remain strictly constrained to 1:1 proportional transforms.

Strictly Prohibited

No Unapproved Color Tinting

Never render the logo in legacy cyan, bright magenta, or neon colors. The approved metallic spectrum is Champagne Gold (#E2C799) and Obsidian Noir (#080706).
