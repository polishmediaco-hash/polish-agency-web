# POLISH Luxury Beauty Copywriting & Funnel Architecture

This skill governs the strategy, voice, and conversion mechanics of all copy, landing page flows, and intake funnels across English, French, and Arabic.

---

## 1. Core Philosophy: Humanized Authority over AI Slop

### 🚫 The Banned "AI Slop" Vocabulary & Formulaic Patterns
1. **Never use formulaic contrasts**: *"We don't just do X, we do Y for dominance."*
2. **Never use generic buzzwords**: *Algorithmic synergy, next-gen, ecosystem, bespoke excellence, revolutionize, fast-paced digital landscape, game-changing, unlock potential.*
3. **Never write bloated paragraphs**: Max **1 to 2 concise sentences** per card description. Cut long paragraphs that cause visual fatigue.
4. **Never talk in abstractions**: Ground every sentence in real cosmetic business realities (formulations, gross margins, TikTok ad creative burn, repeat purchase rate, retail velocity, creator whitelisting).

---

## 2. The Direct-Response Beauty Audit Gates

Every page, section, or funnel step must pass four strict conversion gates:

### Gate A: The 3-Second Founder Value Test
- **Requirement**: An ambitious beauty founder or CMO must understand *what* we do, *who* it is for, and the *outcome* within 3 seconds of landing above the fold.
- **Gold Standard**: *"From Breakthrough Formulation To Household Beauty Brand."* / *"De la Formulation de Rupture à la Marque de Cosmétique Incontournable."*

### Gate B: The Agitation & Mirror Test
Hold up a mirror to the founder's exact operational bottlenecks:
1. **Creative Fatigue**: Ad creatives burning out after 10–14 days, driving CPA up.
2. **Vanity Views vs ROAS**: High-aesthetic UGC videos that generate views but zero cart checkouts.
3. **Low Retention / One-and-Done Buyers**: Failing to convert first-time buyers into 90-day subscription replenishment loops.

### Gate C: The 4-Pillar Growth Engine (Mechanism of Action)
Position solutions as an integrated system, not a generic service checklist:
1. `01 / High-Velocity UGC Studio`: Volume-tested creator content engineered for paid acquisition.
2. `02 / Profit-First Paid Media`: Granular ROAS and CAC compression on Meta & TikTok.
3. `03 / Routine Bundles & AOV`: Regimen-based cross-sells expanding initial order value.
4. `04 / Automated Replenishment & LTV`: Retention email/SMS loops and subscriber loyalty.

### Gate D: Funnel Friction & Momentum Equation
$$\text{Conversion Velocity} = \frac{\text{Perceived Value} + \text{Momentum}}{\text{Cognitive Load} + \text{Unnecessary Fields}}$$

- **Progressive Disclosure**: 3 bite-sized steps beat a 15-field monolith every time.
- **Effortless Micro-Commitments**: Step 1 captures simple baseline info (Brand, Revenue Tier, URL).
- **Single-Click Visual Pills**: Use selectable chips instead of open-ended typing prompts.
- **Micro-Trust Anchors**: Adjacent to every CTA (100% NDA Protocol, 24h Review).

## 3. Trilingual Precision Matrix (EN / FR / AR)

| Component | English (EN) | French Haute Beauté (FR) | Arabic (AR - RTL) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Eyebrow Chip** | Ultra-tight, uppercase (`THE BOTTLENECKS`) | Prestigieux & incisif (`LES FREINS DU DTC BEAUTÉ`) | موجز ومركز (`عقبات النمو`) |
| **Hero Headline** | Assertive outcome, tight cadence | Rythme magistral, vocabulaire cosmétique de pointe | عبارة واثقة وواضحة تركز على النتيجة |
| **Pain Points** | Blunt, metric-aware, commercial urgency | Diagnostic expert, élégance sans lourdeur | تشخيص دقيق لتحديات تكلفة الإعلانات والاحتفاظ |
| **CTAs** | Direct action (`Explore Partnership →`) | Verbe d'action clair (`Découvrir le Partenariat →`) | زر إجراء مباشر (`تقديم طلب الشراكة ←`) |

### Typographic Rules:
- **French**: Always preserve accents on capital letters (`É`, `À`, `È`) and use non-breaking spaces before colons and double punctuation.
- **Arabic**: Ensure natural Tajawal rendering without forced italicization. Arrow vectors flip (`←`).
