Application Notifications

Instant push alerts to your phone or team chat whenever a new application is submitted.

1. Instant Telegram Bot Alerts (Recommended — 100% Free)
Instant Push

Receive an instant Telegram notification on your iPhone/Android with applicant name, brand category, website, and 1-tap WhatsApp reply button as soon as they submit.

How to set up in 2 minutes:
  1. Open Telegram and message @BotFather to create a new bot and copy your TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN.
  2. Message @userinfobot in Telegram to get your numeric TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID.
  3. Add both to your environment variables (or Vercel project settings).
2. Discord or Slack Webhooks
Team Channel

Directly streams leads into your private Discord or Slack channel in real-time. Set WEBHOOK_URL in your environment to activate.

3. Direct WhatsApp Fast-Track (Built-in)
Active

When an applicant submits their brand brief or creator profile, the success page prompts them with a 1-tap WhatsApp button to message you directly with their reference ID.
