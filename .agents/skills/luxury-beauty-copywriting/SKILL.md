# Luxury Beauty Direct-Response Copywriting Framework

## 🎯 Core Philosophy: Humanized Authority over AI Slop

### 🚫 The Banned "AI Slop" Vocabulary & Patterns:
1. **Never use formulaic contrasts**: *"We don't just do X, we do Y for dominance."*
2. **Never use generic buzzwords**: *Algorithmic synergy, next-gen, ecosystem, bespoke excellence, revolutionize, fast-paced digital landscape, game-changing, unlock potential.*
3. **Never use passive, bloated sentences**: Replace *"We evaluate your brand positioning, unit economics, and growth velocity to determine..."* with concrete, direct truths.
4. **Never talk in abstractions**: Ground every sentence in real cosmetic business realities (formulations, gross margins, TikTok ad creative burn, repeat purchase rate, retail velocity, creator whitelisting).

---

## 💎 The 4 Tenets of POLISH Voice & Tone

1. **Operator-to-Founder Resonance**:
   - Speak like an experienced beauty growth partner who has managed seven-figure ad spends, handled supplier timelines, and fixed broken creator briefs.
   - Acknowledge real brand pain points: rising CAC, creative fatigue after 3 weeks, low LTV on first-time beauty buyers, and generic creator content that gets views but zero sales.

2. **Sensory & Tactical Concreteness**:
   - Talk about texture, formula payoff, clinical proof, thumb-stopping unboxings, UGC hook rates, and bottom-line profit.
   - Use sharp, vivid verbs instead of padded adjectives.

3. **Punchy, Varied Cadence**:
   - Alternate short 3-word impact sentences with clear, informative breakdowns.
   - Make copy effortless to skim on mobile and desktop.

4. **Irresistible Value Propositions**:
   - Clearly communicate: Who it's for, what problem it solves, how it works, and the exact next step.
