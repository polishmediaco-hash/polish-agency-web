CARD 02 Algerian Market Realities

Algerian Salon Avatars & Market Realities

Algeria is a cash-driven, relationship-first market where beauty salons generate immense cash during wedding seasons, but suffer from severe visual marketing bottlenecks.

B2B SALON OWNER • HIGH VALUE

Moulat Salon (The Salon Owner)

Owns a multi-chair beauty salon in Algiers, Oran, Constantine, or Setif. Manages 3–10 makeup artists and hair stylists.

  • Core Nightmare: Brides pay 50–80 Mlayen for bridal packages, but phone videos look amateur with messy towels, hair dryers, and sodium yellow lighting.
  • Bottleneck: Has zero time to edit videos or learn complex software like Premiere Pro. Needs fast delegation for staff.
  • Purchasing Power: High cashflow (80–200 Mlayen/month). Readily spends 35,000–95,000 DZD for proven salon prestige.
SOLO CREATOR • VOLUME SCALE

The Freelance Bridal MUA

Independent makeup artist working from home studios or client venues. Charges 25,000–60,000 DZD per bridal booking.

  • Core Nightmare: Loses premium brides to competitors with cinematic 4K Instagram Reels and editorial lighting.
  • Bottleneck: Limited budget for heavy DSLR cameras. Needs professional cinema results strictly using an iPhone.
  • Purchasing Power: Medium (15–40 Mlayen/month). Enrolls immediately if the course pays for itself with 1 single bride.

Payment Rails (No Cards)

90% BaridiMob RIP transfers + 10% Poste Algérie CCP. Zero reliance on international credit cards.

Mlayen Accounting

Salons calculate in centimes ('Mlayen'). 35,000 DZD is framed as 3.5 Mlayen—the price of 1 bridal makeup.

Bridal Spending Reality

Algerian weddings cost 150–400 Mlayen. Brides eagerly pay 15,000 DZD for a dedicated cinematic video souvenir.
