## 1. Executive Context & Algerian Market Nuances

### The Market Reality in Algeria (Marché Algérien)
The beauty and aesthetic salon industry in Algeria is one of the most lucrative and cash-flow-heavy consumer sectors, driven by the culturally sacred **"Saison des Mariages" (Algerian Wedding Season)**:
* **The Wedding Economy**: Between May and October (and post-Ramadan), Algerian families spend fortunes on weddings. A bride ("La Mariée") routinely spends between **30 and 80 Mlayen centimes (300,000 to 800,000 DZD)** on her wedding hall, traditional dresses (Tasdira: Karakou, Chedda, Caftan), and jewelry.
* **The Bridal Makeup Package ("Pack Mariée")**: High-end salons and freelance MUAs in Algiers (Hydra, Sidi Yahia, Cheraga, Kouba), Oran, Constantine, and Sétif charge **25,000 DZD to 70,000 DZD (2.5 to 7 Mlayen)** per bride.
* **The Glaring Pain Point**: Despite charging millions of centimes, salons still film their brides with harsh, unflattering ring lights and personal phones under flickering yellow salon neon lights. The foundation looks cakey, the highlighter reflects blinding white glare, and the videos look cheap on Instagram.
* **Payment Rails**: 95% of e-learning and online course purchases in Algeria occur via **BaridiMob RIP transfer** or **Versement CCP**. WhatsApp screenshot validation is the default checkout standard.
