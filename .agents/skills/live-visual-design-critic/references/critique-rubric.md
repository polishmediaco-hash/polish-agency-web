# Luxury Design Critic Rubric (Awwwards & Haute Parfumerie Standards)

## 1. Spatial Rhythm & Monolith Presence (Weight: 20%)
- **Viewport Command**: Does the hero card command immediate focus as the singular star without feeling cramped or floating aimlessly?
- **Fold Discipline**: Is the hero section 100vh exclusive with 0px peeking from subsequent sections?
- **Breathing Room**: Are inner padding ratios comfortable (60-80px desktop, 28-36px mobile)?
- **Border Radii Harmony**: Consistent corner curvature (36px desktop cards, 26px mobile, 9999px pills).

## 2. Haute Luxury Typography (Weight: 25%)
- **Two-Font Dynamic**: Geometric grotesque (Plus Jakarta Sans) anchor paired with editorial high-fashion italic serif (Cormorant Garamond).
- **Optical Balance**: Does the serif line match the visual x-height of the sans line (1.06x - 1.08x sizing)?
- **RTL Graceful Degradation**: Arabic typography must never use broken italic angles; it must render in natural horizontal cursive geometry.
- **Micro-Copy Legibility**: Badges and pill tags must never drop below 11px and should use uppercase tracking (+0.08em).

## 3. Glassmorphism & Caustic Depth (Weight: 20%)
- **Refraction Balance**: Glass panels must remain translucent enough to reveal floating 3D cosmetic artifacts behind them while keeping white text 100% legible.
- **Specular Rim Highlights**: 1px top bevel highlight (`rgba(255,255,255,0.25 - 0.35)`) and subtle cyan perimeter radiance.
- **Zero Cutouts**: Bounding boxes must blend seamlessly with zero rectangular clipping artifacts on any device.

## 4. Conversion Architecture & Ergonomics (Weight: 20%)
- **Thumb Zone Optimization**: Primary action buttons must sit within natural thumb reach on mobile (48px min touch height).
- **Zero Clutter**: Forms and cards must be stripped of redundant explanation, double arrows, and confusing dual-CTA pointers.
- **Direct Founder Action**: WhatsApp floating trigger must remain accessible without obstructing primary conversion CTAs.

## 5. Multilingual Polish (Weight: 15%)
- **French (FR)**: Account for +15-20% text expansion; verify no awkward wrapping or hyphenation breaks.
- **Arabic (AR)**: Complete layout inversion (RTL), proper font weight preservation, reversed progress bars, and flipped navigation arrows.
