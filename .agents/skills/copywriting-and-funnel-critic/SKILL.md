# Copywriting & Funnel Building Critic Skill

This skill governs the evaluation, distillation, and optimization of all copy, landing page flows, and conversion funnels.

---

## Core Pillars of High-Converting Luxury Copy & Funnels

### 1. The Ruthless Fluff Elimination Test
- **Rule**: If a sentence or clause contains generic buzzwords ("cutting-edge solutions", "synergistic innovation", "transforming the paradigm") without concrete meaning, **strike it immediately**.
- **Rule**: Every line must do exactly one job:
  1. State an undeniable customer problem / agitation.
  2. Prove a proprietary solution / mechanism.
  3. Lower friction to the next logical conversion step.
- **Rule**: Max **1 to 2 concise sentences** per card description. Cut long paragraphs that cause visual fatigue.

### 2. Cognitive Load & Scanning Architecture
- Executive visitors (founders, CMOs, top creators) **scan**, they do not read novels.
- Structure all sections with a rigid 3-tier hierarchy:
  - **Eyebrow Chip**: High-contrast, 2-4 word categorization (e.g., `GROWTH STUDIO`, `THE PROBLEM`).
  - **Headline**: High-contrast, bold, punchy statement (e.g., `Scale Without Burning Out.`).
  - **Supporting Line**: Max 20-25 words of crisp explanation.

### 3. Funnel Flow & Friction Reduction
- **Forms**:
  - Labels must be direct (e.g., `Brand Name`, `Monthly Revenue`, `Instagram / TikTok Link`).
  - Eliminate redundant sub-hints that repeat the label or clutter the UI.
  - Multi-step flows must have rapid 1-click option selections instead of lengthy text prompts.
- **CTAs**:
  - Single, unified action verb (e.g., `Explore Partnership →`, `Join Creator Network →`).
  - Clear expectation of what happens after clicking (e.g., "Direct WhatsApp Priority Line" or "24h Dossier Review").

### 4. Bilingual Precision (EN & FR)
- **English**: Direct, punchy, assertive direct-response luxury agency tone.
- **French**: High-end Parisian luxury tone (*Haute Beauté*, *Performance & Studio Créateurs*), strictly avoiding clunky literal translations and retaining rapid, crisp cadence.

---

## Audit Checklist

When auditing any page or component:
- [ ] Is there any paragraph longer than 2 sentences? (If yes, trim by 50%).
- [ ] Are form labels clean and uncluttered?
- [ ] Is the primary value proposition instantly obvious above the fold in < 3 seconds?
- [ ] Does every CTA lead to the simplest conversion path without unnecessary friction?
