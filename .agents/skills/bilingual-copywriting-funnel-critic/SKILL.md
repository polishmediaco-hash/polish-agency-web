# Bilingual Copywriting & Funnel Building Critic (EN / FR)

This skill provides a rigorous framework for evaluating, auditing, and rewriting luxury direct-response web funnels, landing pages, and multi-step intake experiences for beauty and skincare brands.

---

## 1. The Direct-Response Beauty Audit Heuristic

Every page and section must pass five strict direct-response gates:

### Gate A: The 3-Second Founder Value Test
- **Question**: Does an ambitious beauty brand founder or CMO know *exactly* what we do, *who* it is for, and *why* we are different within 3 seconds of landing?
- **Anti-Pattern**: Generic agency claims like "We transform beauty brands through innovative digital synergy".
- **Gold Standard**: Direct, outcome-driven proposition: *"From Breakthrough Formulation to Household Beauty Brand."* / *"De la Formulation de Rupture à la Marque de Cosmétique Incontournable."*

### Gate B: The Agitation & Diagnosis Gate (The "Mirror" Test)
- **Question**: Does the copy hold up a mirror to the founder's exact 2:00 AM problems?
  1. Creative fatigue (TikTok/Meta ads dying after 14 days).
  2. Empty aesthetic views that fail to generate profitable ROAS.
  3. One-and-done buyers that destroy lifetime customer value (LTV).
- **Metric**: Zero philosophical fluff. State the cost of inaction clearly.

### Gate C: The Proprietary Growth Engine (Mechanism of Action)
- **Question**: Does the copy present our solution as an indispensable, four-pillar growth engine rather than a generic service checklist?
  1. High-Velocity UGC Studio (Creative testing volume).
  2. Profit-First Paid Media (Unit economics & scale).
  3. Routine Bundles & AOV Optimization (Cart value expansion).
  4. Automated Replenishment & Retention (LTV loops).

### Gate D: The Funnel Friction & Momentum Equation
- **Formula**: `Conversion Velocity = (Perceived Value + Momentum) / (Cognitive Load + Unnecessary Fields)`
- **Rules**:
  - Progressive disclosure: 3 bite-sized steps beat a 15-field monolith every time.
  - Step 1 must always be effortless micro-commitments (Name, Brand, Social link).
  - Single-click visual pills for categories and roles.
  - Micro-trust signals (100% NDA protocol, 24h review) adjacent to every submission trigger.

### Gate E: Bilingual Haute Beauté Precision (EN vs. FR)

| Component | English Direct-Response Tone | French Haute Beauté Luxury Tone |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Eyebrow** | Ultra-tight, uppercase chip (e.g. `THE BOTTLENECKS`) | Prestigieux & incisif (e.g. `LES FREINS DU DTC BEAUTÉ`) |
| **Hero Headline** | Bold, active cadence, direct outcome | Rythme magistral, vocabulaire cosmétique de pointe |
| **Pain Points** | Blunt, metric-aware, commercial urgency | Diagnostic expert, élégance percutante sans lourdeur |
| **Pillars** | Clear mechanisms, commercial proof points | Piliers de croissance, expertise studio et formulation |
| **CTAs** | Action-oriented with arrow (`Explore Partnership →`) | Verbe d'action clair & valorisant (`Découvrir le Partenariat →`) |

## 2. Copy Review Matrix & Redlines

When auditing text:
1. **Strike Wordiness**: Eliminate phrases like "in order to", "aims to provide", "seamlessly integrating".
2. **Sharpen Verbs**: Use "Scale", "Engineer", "Orchestrate", "Maximize", "Accelerate". In French: "Accélérer", "Orchestrer", "Déployer", "Pérenniser".
3. **Punctuation & Accents**: Ensure flawless French typographic rules (non-breaking spaces before colons/guillemets, correct uppercase accents: `É`, `À`, `È`).
4. **Form Labels**: Keep field titles under 4 words.

---

## 3. Execution Protocol

When this critic audits a page:
1. Identify all fluff or low-conversion elements.
2. Produce side-by-side English and French revisions.
3. Verify interactive components (toggle responsiveness, progress bars, error handling).
4. Validate live visual rendering on mobile & desktop.
